## 1. How a job comes to you

**Q1 — Main clients, jobs per month?**
The main clients are the actual contractors, and then about 20 jobs and growing.

**Q2 — What do you receive when a job comes in?**
We receive a WhatsApp message from the sales rep of the contractor company, a GC, and they say
"Hey, we just sold a job with a kitchen at this and this address." Then a few things happen.
One, we send out the designer as fast as we can to do the 3D design and the layout. After that
they choose if we do the full kitchen — which includes demo, plumbing, electrical, inspections,
cabinets and countertops — or if we just do the kitchen. They call us in for the design, and
then for the actual install of the cabinets. Sometimes they'll even have a design before, so
maybe just have an "upload design" or "design completed" button.

**Q3 ★ — Who sets the price?**
The cost is determined once we send over the design that we made to our vendors. Then they give
us a price on the cabinet cost etc. to do the whole job. We put our margin on it and send it
back to the actual GC sales rep that we're talking to.

**Q6 — Full remodel vs install only?**
About 50/50, but it doesn't matter. You never know.

## 2. How you price a job

**Q8 — Starting point for price?**
It's a flat number that we give the GC sales rep or the GC themselves — depends who we're
talking to. Most of the time it's the sales rep. A flat number for the whole kitchen. If it's
install only, a flat number. If it's everything together, also a flat number.

**Q9 ★ — What makes the price go up or down?**
Location/distance, if it's far. The finish material could be a little more expensive. But once
we look at the design, most of the time we already know and give a flat rate.

**Q11 — Do you charge for materials?**
When we give a price, it's all in one price.

**Q12 — If you supply anything, how is it priced?**
It's a one-stop shop. From the design point all the way down to whatever they choose — the whole
kitchen, or just cabinet and countertop install.

**Q13 — Template visit charged separately?**
The design is included in the cost, but it determines the cost of the whole job.

**Q14 — Minimum job size?**
Not at this point.

**Q15 — How often does a job cost more than quoted?**
It doesn't happen often. If they add things without us knowing, we make sure we're following the
design. The whole point is that the design is fixed — we stay with that design. If there's a
change, then it's a change order.

## 3. The Google Sheet

**Q16 ★ — Can we get a copy?**
I don't want to ask them that. It's pretty much just a Google Sheet where they upload the design.
It's a shared sheet, and the cabinet provider (a vendor) gives its price for that kind of kitchen.
Sometimes the actual crew doing everything A to Z gives a price according to that design — they
see the design, "okay, this is X amount," and then we know how much it's going to cost us.

**Q19 — What do you wish it did?**
It could be smarter than a Google Sheet. Maybe a portal — "go into the portal, this is the
password," and then just enter a price here. Same thing as the Google Sheet, but in the system.
A portal for vendors. I click "Portal" on the menu and see what they answered for each job we
need a bid for. On their end it's one page, and that's where they bid.

## 4. Your crews

**Q21 — How many crews?**
The people change. We have multiple. I don't have a number because we keep getting more crews,
so it doesn't really matter — we'll always have someone to send to the job.

**Q22 — Employees or subs?**
They are subs.

**Q23 ★ — How is crew pay worked out?**
It changes; each job is different. The materials change, so it depends on that, and on the
destination, if it's far or not far. Once we get the price from the vendor, we know how much it's
going to cost us.

**Q24 — Does that number change by distance, difficulty, crew?**

**Q25 — When do crews get paid?**
The client pays the GC we're doing the work for, but we pay in milestones. Once a design is done,
once a specific step is done — if it's a full remodel, after demo, whatever it is. It's a
milestone situation. We break up the invoices according to that.

**Q26 — Do crews pay for anything themselves?**
They pay for everything themselves. We have the sub that does A to Z, so we don't calculate that.

**Q27 — Who loses money on a callback?**
We pay for the fixes, but we don't need to track it. Not worried about the cost, because most of
the time the sub will have to take that hit.

**Q28 — How do you decide which crew gets which job?**
Depends on the size of the job, and where it is.

**Q29 — Do you hire extra help for a big week?**
No, they manage it all themselves. That's why we're building the system — so they can manage it
from a computer.

## 5. Scheduling

**Q30 — How far ahead do you know the schedule?**
Sometimes one day in advance, sometimes a week. If a sales rep sends us a new job it could be the
day after — "can I get the designer out there tomorrow?" Or it's sold and we need to demo ASAP if
they want the whole thing, because it's all about right away.

**Q31 ★ — What stops a job from getting scheduled?**
Almost nothing, except maybe inspection, and our own scheduling for the day. Maybe we'll push a
job off because another job needs more attention. Most of the time nothing happens except
inspections not passing, and that hardly happens.

**Q32 ★ — Template to countertop install?**
We're mostly working with prefabricated stuff, so sometimes we'll have them install all the
cabinets in the morning and the countertops in the afternoon. Our fastest job was five days —
demo, plumbing and electrical inspection, then cabinets and countertops all at once. Fastest was
five days, longest about two weeks.

**Q33 — How often does a scheduled job move?**
I don't know.

**Q34 — Two jobs in a day per crew?**
It's possible — one in the morning and one in the afternoon — but we usually just leave it for
a day.

**Q36 — Saturdays?**
Yes.

**Q37 — When something moves, who is told?**
The sales rep is updated, and the client, which is our client. We don't talk with the end client;
we talk with the GC rep. Essentially the client is the GC. Within that client there's a rep we
talk to. When we're tracking clients we're tracking companies, and in those companies there are
sales reps.

## 6. Full remodel jobs

**Q38 — Which trades in-house vs subbed?**
It's all in-house — we do the demo, the electrical and plumbing, everything. We're not subbing
anything out.

**Q39 — How are regular subs paid?**
By job.

**Q40 — Who pulls permits?**
The client pulls it — the GC pulls it — but we'll do the inspection. We schedule it and do it.

**Q41 — How do you track inspections?**
We mark it in the system that it passed, then schedule the next task, and update the sales rep
that the inspection passed.

**Q42 — Remodel start to finish?**
Longest was two weeks, fastest was five days.

**Q43 — Paid along the way?**
Depends on the job. Full remodel, we do milestone payments.

## 7. Getting paid

**Q44 ★ — When do you invoice?**
Milestones, as above.

**Q45 — What has to be on the invoice?**
Just a standard invoice — the details, nothing else, because we're sending it to a business.

**Q46 — Payment terms?**
Depends on the job. We build out the milestones; it depends what kind of job it is, full remodel
or just cabinets and countertop install.

**Q48 — How do you track who owes you?**
Unpaid invoices.

**Q49 — Deposit before starting?**
We'll send out an invoice the second the designer is there. We schedule the designer, and
sometimes we schedule the designer the same day, then it'll be sent out.

## 8. Extra work

**Q51 — Crew finds extra work?**
They let us know over WhatsApp that it's an extra expense, and then we send it over to the sales
rep.

**Q52 — Do you charge for it?**
Most likely we send a change order, or over WhatsApp that it's more expensive — it's X amount —
and then they approve it over text.

**Q54 — Unpaid extra work per year?**
None.

## 9. WhatsApp today

**Q55 — How many groups?**
About 40 groups. Each project has one group with the sales rep and one group for the technicians.
We're the intermediary in the middle.

**Q57 — What do you scroll back for most?**
The design and prices.

**Q58 — What gets lost?**
Sometimes approval of schedule. If we say "the guys will be there in the morning" — we want to
automate that. Once we get approved and the schedule goes through on our end, it should update
the sales rep with something like "the guys will be there tomorrow at 8:00," because
communication is key.

**Q59 — Same message to crew every time?**
No, it's just a conversation on WhatsApp — updating them, sending pictures, asking questions.

## 10. You two, and the system

**Q61 — How do you split the work?**
Mostly being on the phone and WhatsApp, talking to the technicians and the sales reps all day.
It's an intermediary role, we're in the middle.

**Q62 — Who uses the system daily?**
Just the two owners. Then we're going to add two data loggers — two receptionists.

**Q63 — Phone or laptop?**
Mostly laptop right now. Focus on the web.

**Q64 — Crew access eventually?**
Eventually we'll send them a link per job. In the WhatsApp group there'd be a pinned link they go
to and just upload pictures of the job. That link is specific to each job. The link itself opens
and uploads pictures or an update, and it automatically sends it to the right project in the
system.

**Q65 — Do clients ask for updates they could see themselves?**
Yes. Maybe some automation where we send them updates in the WhatsApp group. If we scheduled the
guys for 8:00 and approved it early in the morning, we'll have all these messages ready to shoot
out. We go over it and say "okay, yeah" — the guys will be there at 8:00 a.m. at this job — and
then it sends automatically to the sales rep for that specific job.

**Q66 — What software do you pay for?**
Currently nothing.

**Q67 ★ — Fix one thing?**
We need to automate the invoicing and the communication.

**Q68 — What didn't work before?**
Getting off of WhatsApp is very hard. We have to use the same flow but make it smart on our end,
because the sales reps and the technicians won't get off WhatsApp, and you can't change that.
