## The ten questions

### 1. ★ Open the Dashboard. What's missing?

It's 7am, before you start making calls. What do you need on that screen that isn't
there? Name three things.

*Why: this is the screen you'd live on, and right now we guessed at it.*

### 2. ★ Which of these do you actually want? Tick the ones you'd use.

Don't be polite — every one you tick is a week we spend not building something else.

- [ ] Jobs on a **map**
- [ ] **Reports** — how last month went
- [ ] **Profit per job** — what we made after paying everyone
- [ ] **Expenses** we pay directly (permits, dumpsters, materials we buy)
- [ ] **Leads** — jobs before the GC has sold them
- [ ] **Month calendar** instead of / as well as the week view
- [ ] **Document storage** — designs, permits, contracts on the job
- [ ] **Search** across everything
- [ ] Somewhere to keep **notes** on a job
- [ ] **Time tracking** for crews
- [ ] Something else: ______________

### 3. ★ The design — where does it live?

Right now the app records *that* a design is done but doesn't hold the file.

Where are your 3D designs today — a PDF, a link to some software, screenshots in
WhatsApp? Do you need to open the design inside the app, or is a link enough?

*Why: it's the thing you scroll back for most, and vendors price off it.*

### 4. Money going out — is it only the crew?

Round one said the subs pay for everything themselves. Since then: do you ever pay for
anything directly on a job? Permits, dumpsters, appliances, a delivery, a designer?

If yes, roughly how many of those a month?

### 5. Before it's a job

A rep tells you about a kitchen that isn't sold yet. Does that live anywhere today, or
do you only hear about jobs once they're sold?

*Why: it's the difference between the app starting at "sold" or earlier.*

### 6. What do you want to know at the end of the month?

Name the three numbers. Jobs done? Money in? Average job size? What we made? Which GC
sends the most work?

*Why: it tells us what to record now. Anything we don't capture, we can't report on later.*

### 7. Phone or laptop — honestly?

You said laptop. But when you're out, do you need to look something up, or send an
update, from your phone? Which two or three screens?

*Why: the app has no phone menu yet. We'll build one — we just need to know how far it goes.*

### 8. The two receptionists

When they start, what do you actually want them doing in here? Creating jobs, chasing
vendors for prices, sending invoices, logging photos?

And confirm: they shouldn't see what the crews charge or what we make — right?

### 9. What are you still doing outside the app?

After a week of using it, what are you still doing in WhatsApp, on paper, or in your
head that the app should be doing?

*Why: the honest gap list, and probably the most useful answer in this whole document.*

### 10. ★ If this saved you one hour a day, which hour?

The one job you'd hand over first. Chasing vendors for prices? Writing the same
schedule message forty times? Working out who owes you money? Something else?

*Why: it tells us what to make excellent, versus merely present.*

## Two things we still need from round one

Small, but they're holding up real work:

**★ Is 50% markup or 50% margin?** On a $10,000 cost: 50% *markup* means you charge
$15,000 and make 33%. 50% *margin* means you charge $20,000 and make 50%. Which one?
(The app shows both numbers so you can't get it wrong — we just need to know what to
set as the default.)

**Milestone amounts.** The app splits the price evenly across milestones as a
placeholder. What should it actually be? For example, on a full remodel: how much at
design, after demo, after cabinets, at the end?
